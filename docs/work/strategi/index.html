    The challenge

    One brand, many kinds of content

    Constant production, with content of different kinds, had to be recognized as a single brand.

    Grupo Strategi operates across five distinct business lines. Each has its own audience, type of message and communication pace, yet all of them needed to be recognized as part of the same brand, whether on social media, in presentations to clients and partners, or at events.

    However, the communication was becoming outdated as the brand expanded, and the existing patterns could not keep up with the variety of content each area required.

    What needed to change

    The language had to be versatile enough to sustain constant production. On social media, it had to avoid monotony in a feed with diverse topics and, more broadly, convey a premium perception worthy of a high-value technology provider across every brand touchpoint.

    01

    System decisions

    System

    Instead of designing isolated pieces, the work started by defining a system: a set of elements and rules that could be reapplied at any touchpoint.

    Anatomy

    Six elements, one piece

    The piece on the left brings the system’s elements together; on the right, each graphic and texture appears on its own, with its role.

    Decision 01

    Graphics

    The system relies on three graphics with distinct roles, shown on their own in the anatomy (FIG. 01). With well-defined roles, they can be combined in different ways without losing coherence, which helps avoid repetition in the feed.

    Decision 02

    Palette and textures

    The palette is restrained, with a graphite blue as the base, the brand’s orange as the accent and a grayish blue in support. Which color dominates depends on the post pattern: sometimes orange, sometimes graphite blue or white. Wave-line and grain textures, shown in the anatomy (FIG. 01), complete the language.

    Decision 03

    Typography and hierarchy

    Poppins, a geometric sans-serif, takes the headings in bold; Inter carries the supporting text and complementary information. The clear hierarchy ensures legibility in small formats such as posts and keeps a modern, objective tone, consistent with a technology company.

    Decision 04

    Composition

    Pieces combine a rounded card that holds the text with images masked by the brand symbol. The signature, with logo and tagline in fixed positions, creates a recognizable frame while the center of each piece changes with the message. In carousels, a navigation indicator in the accent color guides reading.

    Decision 05

    Scalability

    Instead of tying each template to a type of content, the system offers a repertoire of visual architectures: the social media team picks the template that best serves each post. The templates were organized into a Figma library made available to the social media team. As a result, the team started producing pieces autonomously, applying the visual language consistently without having to create each piece from scratch.

    Applications

    Beyond the feed

    Beyond social media, the system structured institutional communication and took the brand to events and physical spaces.

    Context 01

    Corporate communication

    The same language shaped presentations and materials used to introduce the brand, its services and its work.

    Context 02

    The brand beyond screens

    Standing displays, booths, banners and signage extended the system to events and physical spaces, connecting digital communication with in-person contact with the brand.

    Results

    Unity, autonomy and premium perception

    A single language for all of the group’s communication, produced with more agility and autonomy by the team, without losing variety.

    01

    One language for five business lines

    The visual system now supports communication for all five Grupo Strategi business lines with a single language, recognizable at every touchpoint: social media, presentations to clients and partners, events and physical spaces.

    02

    Autonomy and speed

    With the Figma template library, the social media team gained the autonomy to produce content without designing each piece from scratch. Production moved from case-by-case to defined standards, becoming faster and more consistent.

    03

    Variety with a premium feel

    The range of layouts made it possible to keep a steady volume of posts on different topics without a repetitive feed, while the restrained palette, textures and graphic elements reinforced the brand’s premium feel.
