    Context and challenge

    From a technical, impersonal language to an experience that connects with entrepreneurs.

    Context

    An operation already at scale

    Sebrae is Brazil’s national agency supporting micro and small businesses. Hub Sebrae RN connects these businesses to consulting services, bringing companies closer to solutions and qualified professionals.

    In 2023, the operation had already reached more than 300,000 hours of consulting and more than 5,000 businesses served.

    300k+consulting hours in 2023
    5k+companies served in 2023

    The starting point

    A structured platform, but distant from entrepreneurs

    The previous experience relied on a predominantly gray, neutral identity, built on visual references to technology and computer circuits. The language reinforced the idea of a platform structured for selecting services, but did little to speak to or guide the entrepreneur on the other side of the screen, who needed to discover, choose and hire those services.

    What needed to change

    What needed to change
    BeforeNew direction
    Institutional languageCloser and more human
    Operation-driven experienceUser-driven journey
    Generic, undifferentiated identityDistinct, recognizable identity
    Perceived complexitySimpler, guided experience

    The design challenge

    How might we shift the Hub from being perceived as a service-selection system to becoming, for entrepreneurs, a clear path to the right consulting service for their business?

    To answer this question, the project was carried out on two connected fronts:

    01

    01 · Design decisions · Front 1

    Identity

    Brand direction, concept, typography and visual system.

    Brand direction

    An identity of its own, connected to Sebrae

    The identity was designed for digital from the start. The goal was a brand with its own personality, yet still recognizable as part of the Sebrae ecosystem.

    Concept and typography1 / 2 →

    The concept

    Individuality + territory + closeness

    The symbol combines a fingerprint with the outline of the state of Rio Grande do Norte.

    1. 1The fingerprint represents the uniqueness of each business.
    2. 2The territory reinforces the local, close nature of the service.

    Typography

    Impact in the brand, legibility in the system

    For the word HUB, Audiowide brings a modern, high-impact approach: its geometric shapes reinforce the idea of innovation and give the brand presence. For the rest of the system, legibility came first: Poppins Bold organizes headings and highlights clearly, and Manrope Regular ensures comfortable reading in body text and interface components.

    Visual system

    A more human, dynamic language

    The visual system combines the Hub’s own elements with references from the Sebrae brand:

    • Fluid shapes
    • Hexagons
    • Fingerprint texture
    • Parallelograms and slanted lines
    • Photography of entrepreneurs
    • Expanded palette

    Palette

    Tied to Sebrae, without the old neutrality

    The palette was built to balance two needs: keeping the link with Sebrae and breaking away from the neutrality of the previous experience.

    Sebrae blue is the foundation of the system. Used in backgrounds, the brand and the main interface elements, it preserves the association with trust and institutional recognition.

    Cyan, green, yellow, lilac and magenta form the supporting palette and represent the diversity of the businesses the Hub serves: companies of different sectors, sizes and realities. Applied to graphics, highlights and navigation elements, these colors bring energy and warmth to the communication and help differentiate content.

    The result is a language that remains recognizable as Sebrae, while leaving behind the gray, cold tone of the previous version to get closer to entrepreneurs.

    Experience

    From identity to product, architecture and the entrepreneur’s journey.

    From identity to experience

    An identity designed to work in the product

    The new identity was not treated as just a visual layer. Its principles — shapes, colors, graphic elements and photographic language — were built into the portal experience to create clearer, more human and consistent communication.

    2. →

    Experience architecture

    One platform. Different needs.

    The Hub connects different players within the same ecosystem. For the public portal, the experience was structured mainly around two goals:

    Entrepreneur

    Find a solution for the business

    1. Search
    2. →
    3. Choose
    4. →
    5. Learn
    6. →
    7. Start hiring
    Provider

    Offer their services

    1. Discover opportunities
    2. →
    3. Accreditation

    The experience architecture was organized so each audience could quickly find its path, without turning the platform into an overly complex interface.

    The public portal covers the journey up to the start of hiring. To complete it, the entrepreneur signs up or logs in to the Hub platform. Providers, in turn, discover opportunities to work with the Hub and start their accreditation to offer services on the platform.

    From discovery to starting to hire

    The entrepreneur’s journey on the portal

    The portal is the Hub’s front door. Its role is to help entrepreneurs find a solution, understand the offer and take the first step to hire it. Each stage of the journey shaped experience and interface decisions, from information hierarchy to navigation, components and calls to action.

    Responsive experience

    The same visual language was adapted to different access contexts, preserving hierarchy, content and main paths on desktop and mobile.

    Results

    From identity to product evolution

    The project had impact on two fronts: it established an identity of the Hub’s own and became the strategic foundation for the product’s evolution.

    01

    Identity

    The Hub gained a proprietary visual system, connected to the Sebrae ecosystem and closer to entrepreneurs, able to adapt to different interfaces and touchpoints. The identity was approved to be incorporated into the SaaS, becoming the visual reference for the entire product.

    02

    Product evolution

    The identity and the portal laid the groundwork for the Hub SaaS redesign proposal, which accompanied the platform’s expansion beyond Rio Grande do Norte.
