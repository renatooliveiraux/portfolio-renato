Hub Sebrae RN Identity and Portal

Brand and portal for a platform that connects micro and small businesses to consulting subsidized by Sebrae RN, with a language closer to the people who run them.

Company
Grupo Strategi + Sebrae RN
Scope
Product Visual Identity · Website/Portal
Year
2024

Context and challenge

From a technological, impersonal language to an experience that connects with the entrepreneur.

Context

Sebrae is Brazil’s national agency supporting micro and small businesses. In the state of Rio Grande do Norte (RN), its Hub connects small businesses with subsidized consulting, bringing companies closer to solutions and accredited professionals.

By 2023, the operation had already reached more than 300,000 consulting hours and more than 5,000 businesses served.

The starting point

The previous experience relied on a predominantly gray, neutral identity built on visual references to technology and computer circuits. The language reinforced the idea of a platform structured for selecting services, but did little to speak to or guide the entrepreneur on the other side of the screen, who needed to discover, choose and hire those services.

What needed to change

The design challenge

How do you create an identity that conveys innovation and closeness without losing the trust associated with Sebrae — and turn a complex operation into a simple, inviting digital experience?

From this challenge, the project was carried out on two connected fronts:

  1. 01 Identity Create a distinctive visual language for the Hub.
  2. 02 Experience Translate that language into a clearer, more accessible digital journey built around users’ goals.

My role

From identity to digital experience

I worked on creating the Hub’s visual identity and designing the portal, following the project from the brand concept to the desktop and mobile interfaces.

Design decisions

01

Brand direction

An identity of its own, connected to Sebrae

The identity was designed for digital from the start. The goal was to create a brand with its own personality, yet still recognizable as part of the Sebrae ecosystem.

The concept

Individuality + territory + closeness

The symbol combines a fingerprint with the outline of Rio Grande do Norte.

The fingerprint stands for the uniqueness of each business, while the territory reinforces the local, close nature of the service.

02

Visual system

A more human, dynamic language

The visual system combines the Hub’s own elements with references from the Sebrae brand:

  • Fluid shapes
  • Hexagons
  • Fingerprint texture
  • Parallelograms and slanted lines
  • Photography of entrepreneurs
  • Expanded palette

Sebrae blue keeps the association with trust and institutional recognition, while cyan, green, yellow, lilac and magenta bring more energy and closeness.

From identity to experience

An identity designed to work in the product

The new identity was not treated as just a visual layer. Its principles — shapes, colors, graphics and photographic language — were built into the portal experience to create clearer, more human and consistent communication.

Experience architecture

One platform. Different needs.

The Hub connects different players within the same ecosystem. For the public portal, the experience was structured mainly around two goals:

Entrepreneur

Find a solution for the business

Search → learn → request → hire

Provider

Offer their services

Discover opportunities → list services → serve

The experience architecture was organized so that each audience could quickly find its way, without turning the platform into an overly complex interface.
